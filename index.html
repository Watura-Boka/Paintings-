About Asaj

I paint the moments that hold still in memory — a certain light, a certain quiet.

I was born in Jaela, Sri Lanka, in 1981, and studied at the University of Colombo. Before painting became my full-time work, I spent five years in marketing and sales within the advertising industry — a career that taught me how to look at things closely, even if it wasn't where I belonged.

I've been exhibiting since 2011, from a group show in Stockholm to "Lankan Art Vista" in Mumbai, and I've shown at Kala Pola, Colombo's open-air art fair, for ten consecutive years. My work has gone on to hotel and residential commissions across Negombo, Colombo, and Anuradhapura, in collaboration with architects including Gayan Herath and Mevan Ranasinghe.

My paintings move between the coast, the hill country, and quieter interior scenes — always in oil or acrylic, always on canvas, always finished with a frame before they leave my hands. I don't reproduce a painting once it's sold. If you own one of my pieces, you own the only one.

Most buyers find a piece here, message me directly, and we take it from there — the way I've always preferred to sell.
